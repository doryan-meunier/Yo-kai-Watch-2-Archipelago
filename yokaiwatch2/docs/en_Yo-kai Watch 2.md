# Yo-kai Watch 2

## What does randomization do to this game?

Yo-kai Watch 2: Psychic Specters becomes a multiworld game: treasure chests,
ground pickups, the game's 83 quests and 10 favors, the 52 Yo-kai hideouts,
the 26 Tablo-blabla riddles, Komasan's adventures, Yo-criminal captures,
bosses, watch ranks, story chapters and (optionally) the insect/fish
collections, befriended Yo-kai, fusion items and legendary seals all become
Archipelago checks (names use the French localization). Key items - the
bicycle, watch rank upgrades, dungeon keys
and tools - are shuffled into the multiworld item pool. Train destinations
(Ourcival, San Fantastico) and the past unlock through story progression,
while reaching the Limbes éternelles or the Paradis divin depends on keys
found by you or other players.

## What is the goal?

Configurable in your YAML:

- **Final Boss** - defeat Lady Démona,
- **Story 100** - complete all 11 story chapters,
- **Infinite Inferno** - defeat Potofeu in the Inferno depths,
- **Divine Paradise** - defeat Filomène,
- **All Legendaries** - collect every Legendary Medal,
- **All Checks** - full clear.

## Which items can be in another player's world?

Progression items (bicycle, watch ranks, passes, tickets, keys, tools,
legendary medals, story chapters when shuffled), useful consumable bundles
(Soul Gems, money, first-aid kits), filler food items, and optional traps.

## What does another world's item look like in Yo-kai Watch 2?

Until the game-side integration is finalized, checks are tracked through the
Yo-kai Watch 2 Client (text client with an emulator memory bridge). Received
items are logged in the client and applied to the game where supported.

## Unique local commands

- `/citra [port]` - attach the client to the Citra/Azahar GDB stub.
